# opposable — Acceptable Use Policy

**Version:** 2026-07-31 · **Status: draft, not reviewed by counsel.** Forms part of the [Terms of Service](TERMS.md).

This policy exists because we hand strangers a Linux machine with a network connection and a model driving it. Breaching it is grounds for **immediate suspension without notice**, and we will cooperate with law enforcement where required.

## The short version

Do not use the agent to do anything you could not lawfully do yourself, and do not use our infrastructure to reach systems you do not own or have written permission to test.

## Prohibited

**Attacking other systems.** Unauthorized access, scanning, credential stuffing, brute-forcing, denial of service, or exploiting vulnerabilities in systems you do not own or have documented authorization to test. "The model decided to" is not a defence — you directed the run.

**Abusing our infrastructure.** Cryptocurrency mining. Proxying or relaying third-party traffic. Bulk unsolicited email (outbound SMTP is blocked permanently and we will not unblock it). Circumventing quotas, sandbox isolation, egress controls, or account limits, including by creating multiple accounts.

**Illegal content and conduct.** Child sexual abuse material; content that sexualises minors; incitement to violence or terrorism; trafficking in stolen data, credentials or controlled goods; fraud; targeted harassment; non-consensual intimate imagery; unlawful discrimination.

**Deception at scale.** Generating content that impersonates a real person or organisation, fabricating records, reviews or credentials presented as genuine, building phishing infrastructure, or producing coordinated inauthentic content for political or commercial manipulation.

**Weapons and critical harm.** Assisting in the design, acquisition or deployment of biological, chemical, radiological or nuclear weapons, or of malware and exploit chains intended for unauthorized use.

**Privacy violations.** Scraping or aggregating personal data without a lawful basis, building surveillance or tracking tooling aimed at individuals, or processing biometric or special-category data without consent.

**High-stakes automated decisions.** Using output as the sole basis for decisions with legal or similarly significant effects on a person — credit, employment, housing, insurance, benefits, medical diagnosis, legal advice — without qualified human review.

**Model-provider policies flow down.** Your provider's usage policy applies to everything you submit through us, including via passthrough access. Where their policy is stricter than this one, theirs governs.

## Security testing

Permitted only against systems you own, or under a written engagement whose scope you can produce on request. If we ask, you must be able to show it. Bug-bounty scope counts as written authorization for the assets it names.

## Rate and volume

Fair use within your plan's sandbox-hours, concurrency and egress limits. Automated retries that ignore an error we return, and workloads engineered to sit permanently at the limit, are abuse regardless of the number.

## Reporting

Abuse reports: **abuse@** *(address to be published at launch)*. We monitor it and aim to acknowledge within one business day.

Copyright: our DMCA designated agent is registered with the US Copyright Office; contact details at `/legal`. *(Registration is $6 and must be renewed every three years — without it there is no §512 safe harbour.)*

## Enforcement

Depending on severity we may: warn; throttle; suspend a task; suspend an account; terminate; or refer to law enforcement. We suspend first and investigate second where there is an active threat to the Service or a third party. Suspension does not entitle you to a refund.

If you believe we got it wrong, reply to the suspension notice and we will look again.
